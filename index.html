Frequently Asked Questions

How does Mingzy help you meet someone new?

Mingzy uses an instant pairing algorithm that matches you 1-on-1 with real users worldwide based on language and mutual availability. There are no profiles, swipes, or waiting rooms.

Is Mingzy 100% free with no registration?

Yes. Mingzy requires zero signup, email confirmation, or credit card. You can start video chatting or text chatting immediately by opening the website.

How do the skip and block safety controls work?

You have total control over every interaction. You can instantly skip to the next stranger by pressing the spacebar or clicking Next. Inappropriate behavior can be reported and blocked with 1 click.

Are video and audio calls encrypted and private?

All video streams run through direct encrypted browser-to-browser channels. Mingzy never records, monitors, or stores your private video calls on central servers.

What devices and web browsers are supported?

Mingzy works seamlessly on Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android without requiring app installs.

Can I chat by text if I do not have a webcam?

Yes. Mingzy includes a dedicated Random Text Chat mode optimized for lightweight messaging, low data usage, and complete anonymity without camera or microphone access.
