Mingzy Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Version 1.0

Welcome to Mingzy. Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy outlines how Mingzy ("we", "our", or "the App") handles user information across our mobile and web applications.

1. Anonymity by Default

Mingzy does not require users to create an account, provide phone numbers, or submit government identification to participate in matchmaking. Connections are established anonymously.

2. Zero Video & Audio Recording

All video and audio communications between users are conducted directly peer-to-peer (P2P) utilizing encrypted WebRTC technology. Our servers act solely as a signaling mediator and never record, monitor, or store any video or audio streams.

3. Ephemeral Text Messages

Chat messages sent during active sessions are stored only in volatile client memory for the duration of the conversation. When either user disconnects, skips to the next match, or exits the room, all message histories for that session are immediately purged.

4. Analytics & Technical Logs

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect minimal, non-identifiable diagnostic metrics to maintain service uptime, optimize matchmaking speed, and diagnose client crashes. This data includes:

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • Session start/end timestamps and call duration.
  • Anonymized error logs and performance statistics.

5. Zero Data Selling

We do not sell, monetize, rent, or trade any user data, identifiers, or technical analytics to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

6. User Safety & Reporting

Users have the ability to report inappropriate conduct via our one-tap reporting tool. In such cases, the anonymous session identifier may be temporarily flagged to enforce bans against malicious actors and protect the safety of the community.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at:
support@mingzy.app
